# Specification 002 — Visual Redesign and Recording Mode

- Document version: 1.0
- Status: Implemented, awaiting owner review (ADR-002 to ADR-006 are `Proposed`)
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Scope: presentation layer (`Nain3D.Client`) and bundled sample models only

## 1. Problem statement

The first screen was the weakest part of the demo: `sample-monitor.glb` was a flat white box of 12
triangles with no materials, lighting or environment. The interface was dense, technical and mixed
Spanish and English. The verdict panel was long and could not be read at a glance. The demo could not be
recorded cleanly for vertical or horizontal social video.

Goal: show something visually striking in 2 s and tell the whole story in 20-30 s
("I upload a model, the AI proposes, the contract decides"). The conformant vs non-conformant contrast is the hook.

## 2. Out of scope (hard limits)

- No change to business rules, API contracts, schemas or the fail-closed principle.
- No new computation of business figures in the client. Every number shown (triangles, extents, limits,
  verdict, SHA-256, latency) comes from `EvaluationResult` / `ProfileDefinition`.
- No paid services, no secrets, no personal data, no employer material.

## 3. Requirements (EARS)

### 3.1 Hero models
- **REQ-VR-01** When the page loads, the system shall display a hero model with PBR materials, a studio
  environment, soft shadows and eased rotation within 2 s on a mid-range laptop.
- **REQ-VR-02** The bundled samples shall be richer models generated by code, covering the existing four
  sample ids and their expected verdicts.
- **REQ-VR-03** Sample generation shall be deterministic (same bytes for the same source) and free of cost.

### 3.2 Visible defects
- **REQ-VR-04** When the verdict is `NON_CONFORMANT` because of `metric_scale`, the viewport shall draw the
  model bounding box in red, the profile limit as a reference box, and a scale ruler, using only
  `features.extents`, `applied_profile.max_dimension_m` and the API's check result.
- **REQ-VR-05** When a mesh defect (holes, degenerate faces) is the cause, the defect shall be visible in the
  model itself (authored into a sample) and named by the failing check; the client shall not compute face
  positions. **Deferred** (section 5).

### 3.3 Verdict reveal
- **REQ-VR-06** When an evaluation completes, the checks shall be marked one by one, then the green or red
  badge shall appear with motion, then the latency counter (`evaluation_duration_ms`).
- **REQ-VR-07** The user shall be able to switch between a conformant and a non-conformant sample with one
  gesture (button and the `C` key).
- **REQ-VR-08** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the reveal shall be instantaneous and rotation off.

### 3.4 Recording mode
- **REQ-VR-09** When `?mode=record` is present, the system shall hide the page chrome, use large type, a fixed
  viewport, deterministic animation timing and no hover dependence.
- **REQ-VR-10** The recording layout shall fit 1080x1920 (9:16, respecting platform safe zones) and 1920x1080
  without scrolling.
- **REQ-VR-11** Recording mode shall work in Spanish and English.

### 3.5 Identity and languages
- **REQ-VR-12** The UI shall use NainDev brand tokens from `brandbook/tokens.css`.
- **REQ-VR-13** Spanish (Spain) is the authoritative source language; English is optional from a reviewed
  catalogue. Technical terms without an equivalent remain in English in both languages.
- **REQ-VR-14** When `?lang=en` is present the UI shall render in English; otherwise Spanish. The choice
  shall be remembered in `localStorage` only (no cookies) and a visible selector shall switch it.
- **REQ-VR-15** A test shall fail if an English key is missing or extra relative to the Spanish source, and if
  a component template carries literal user-facing text.

### 3.6 Quality
- **REQ-VR-16** WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled controls, live-region
  announcement of the verdict, `lang` attribute kept in sync.
- **REQ-VR-17** Baseline and final LCP and bundle size shall be measured and recorded in `docs/`.
- **REQ-VR-18** Playwright captures of: start, conformant, non-conformant, recording 1080x1920 and
  1920x1080, in both languages.

## 4. Pre-existing defect found

`npm run build` failed on `master` (`src/services/api.ts:92` read `result.passed`, absent from
`EvaluationResult`). Fixed in T-001 (telemetry payload only).

## 5. Findings and open points

1. **REQ-VR-05 deferred.** 3d.naindev.com is static: verdicts come from `mockEvaluator.ts`, a heuristic that
   reports zero degenerate faces and derives watertightness from the triangle count. Only the Python engine can
   fail mesh-defect checks, and its API returns no face positions. A defect demo needs an owner decision
   (a real engine behind the demo, or a new API field, which is a contract change).
2. The in-browser evaluator proposes `Pieza Industrial` for any watertight mesh over 1,000 triangles, so the
   non-industrial samples stay under 1,000 triangles (ADR-003).
3. `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` still name `<model-viewer>`; they change only after ADR-002 is approved.
4. Captures weigh about 15 MB in git (T-023).
