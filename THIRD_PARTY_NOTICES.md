## 3D model

| Asset | Files | Author | Licence | Source |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Materials Variants Shoe | `Nain3D.Client/public/samples/sample-shoe.glb`, `sample-oversized-shoe.glb` | Shopify (2021) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | [KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/MaterialsVariantsShoe) |

Changes made to the model: one material variant kept, transforms baked into the vertices, textures resized
and re-encoded as JPEG, unused data pruned, and (for `sample-oversized-shoe.glb`) uniformly scaled to a 5 m
largest dimension. The reproducible pipeline is `npm --prefix Nain3D.Client run optimize:models`. The credit
is also shown inside the 3D frame of the demo.

The other four sample models (valve, monitor, chair, giant chair) are generated by code in this repository
(`Nain3D.Client/tools/generate-samples.mjs`) and are MIT licensed.
